# Privacy Policy for Replyvora

*Last updated: August 30, 2026*

Replyvora ("the extension") is a browser extension that analyzes open Gmail messages and generates contextual, editable reply drafts.

## Data Processing

Replyvora processes email content entirely within your browser. The content of the email you are viewing is read locally in order to generate a reply suggestion. This content is not transmitted to, or stored on, any external server.

## Data We Store

The extension uses Chrome's local storage to save your preferences (such as reply tone and auto-analyze settings) and your consent status. This data stays on your device and is not shared with any third party.

## Data We Do Not Collect

Replyvora does not collect, transmit, or sell personal identification information, health information, financial information, authentication credentials, location data, browsing history, or user activity data.

## Third Parties

Replyvora does not share, sell, or transfer any user data to third parties.

## Permissions

The extension requests access only to mail.google.com, which is required to read the currently open email and generate a reply draft. No other websites are accessed.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page.
