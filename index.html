Privacy policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Replyvora ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that helps you read and reply to emails inside Gmail (https://mail.google.com/*).

What Replyvora accesses

  • Email content you have open in Gmail. When you open an email, Replyvora reads the visible sender, subject, and body of that message directly from the Gmail page, so it can classify the message and draft a suggested reply.
  • Your extension settings (enabled/disabled, reply tone, auto-analyze), stored locally using Chrome's storage API.

What Replyvora does not do

  • It does not send your emails, drafts, or any other personal data to any external server — all analysis and draft generation happen entirely on your device.
  • It does not use any remote AI/LLM API. Suggested replies are generated locally using keyword-based rules.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, and does not use your data for advertising.
  • It does not track you across other websites.

Data storage and retention

Your settings are stored locally in your browser and never leave your device. Email content is read only while the relevant Gmail tab is open and is not persisted anywhere by the extension.

Permissions

  • storage — to remember your preferences (tone, auto-analyze, enabled).
  • Host permission for https://mail.google.com/* — so the extension can run on Gmail and read the currently open email.
